Deep Space

Deep space missions demand strict adherence to environmental protection, sustainability, and operational resilience. Below is an overview of the core categories required for low‑cost, high‑feasibility mission success.

Deep Space Requirements

  1. Elemental Protection
    • Ionizing radiation
    • Non-ionizing radiation
    • Kinetic elements
    • External pressure
  2. Sustainable Atmosphere
    • Oxygen supplies
    • CO₂ scrubbers
    • Internal pressure regulation
    • Nitrogen supplies
    • Dehumidifiers
    • Biofilters
    • Disinfectants
    • Maintenance protocols
  3. Sustainable Power Supply
    • Solar systems
    • Additional proprietary power systems
  4. Physical Strengthening Systems
    • Low‑cost atrophy prevention systems
  5. Cognitive & Mental Health Systems
    • Cognitive resilience systems
  6. Sensor Systems
    • All necessary deep‑space sensors
  7. Sustainable Fuel Supplies
    • Fuel sustainability systems
  8. Satellite Retrieval Systems
    • External modules for debris collection and maintenance

Deep Space Standards of Protocol

1.1 Ionizing Radiation Protection

1.1.1 Galactic Cosmic Rays (GCRs)
  • Protons
  • Alpha particles
  • HZE ions
1.1.2 Solar Particle Events (SPEs)
  • Protons
  • Alpha particles
  • Trace heavy ions
1.1.3 Secondary Radiation
  • Neutrons
  • Bremsstrahlung X-rays
  • Nuclear fragments
  • Electrons & positrons

1.2 Non-Ionizing Radiation

  • Ultraviolet (UV)
  • Infrared (IR)
  • Visible light (thermal)

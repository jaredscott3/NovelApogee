What Is Novel Apogee?

Novel Apogee is an aerospace guidance firm focused on mission feasibility, risk reduction, and operational support across launch, re-entry, and deep-space environments. We turn high-risk challenges into manageable mission strategies through targeted analysis and engineering insight.

What We Do

  • Guide launch & re-entry thermal methodology
  • Provide deep-space radiation & sustainment management
  • Perform mission feasibility analysis
  • Offer risk-reduction and operational guidance

Why We Exist

Space missions often face avoidable thermal, radiation, and operational challenges that inflate cost and risk. Novel Apogee exists to simplify these challenges, making space travel more affordable, more feasible, and more operationally resilient.

Our Approach

We convert high-risk technical problems into low- to medium-risk mission strategies. Through study, analysis, and engineering guidance, we help organizations reduce overall costs by increasing simplicity, reliability, and operational capability.

Who We Serve

  • Aerospace organizations
  • Space companies
  • Research institutions
  • Engineering teams

Origin of Novel Apogee

I am Jared Scott, founder of Novel Apogee. With a background spanning communications networking, radio transmissions, UAV operations, and intelligence analysis, I’ve seen firsthand how man-hours, lives, and trillions of dollars are lost in the pursuit of space.

Novel Apogee was created to help prevent future failures and reduce unnecessary costs. My goal is to provide guidance shaped by experience, helping humanity advance to the stars safely, affordably, and sustainably.
